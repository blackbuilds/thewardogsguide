The reserve rule: don’t spend the bankroll

Public guides (games.gg money, PixelNitro / DaOpa-class, Overgear) share the same philosophy: do not dump the entire journey bankroll into the first life. This page adopts that as a process, not a fake dollar reserve. games.gg core rule paraphrase: spend less on a life than you expect to earn back.

Process starter (no prices):

  1. Do not dump the $10,000 journey bankroll on life one (Steam store About, 2026-09-13).
  2. Buy only what you will use this life: a primary you can control, matching ammo, and a survive / revive tool if cash remains.
  3. Skip vehicles, FOB kits, and heavy armor until you have completed 2–3 deaths without going broke.
  4. If you touch the Hot Zone (store: double cash), keep the kit cheap — dying with an expensive loadout inside a contested multiplier is negative EV.

Earn checklist (first funded hour)

  1. Complete deploy with ammo bought.
  2. Play the objective rules on Maps (Control Zone points) instead of pure deathmatch.
  3. Revive / transport / teamplay actions if they pay.
  4. Touch Hot Zone only with a reserve and a squad plan.
  5. After each death: rebuy under reserve; screenshot if prices confuse you.
  6. Never empty wallet to copy a streamer’s mid-game kit.
  7. Skip Oil Rig until you can name Build + Fuel delivery plan.

FAQ

Q: How much cash does a new player start with on EA?
A: Steam store About (2026-09-13): journey starts with $10,000 — store-explicit. Once / per match / post-wipe on HUD remains a public lead. Do not silently adopt “every match” third-party wording.

Q: Does cash persist between matches, and is there an EA wipe?
A: Steam store About (2026-09-13): cash persists match to match — store-explicit. Wipe / season rules: public lead.

Q: How do I keep money match to match?
A: Earn via objectives/teamplay/Hot Zone , spend under a reserve, accept that death may cost the kit. Process > copying a $3,600 blog core.

Q: What is a safe under-budget starter kit?
A: Fill the price table with live vendor screenshots and a stated budget/role. Until then, ultra-budget + ammo-first (primary, mags, loose ammo, bandages).

Q: Is ammo purchased separately?
A: Community leads say yes; we omit the exact vendor UI path until confirmed. Indie lead (games.gg): refill from cover.

Q: What is the best first gun?
A: No undated “T-21 is best” on this page. Any recommendation needs build/date + budget/role + live price after you capture the vendor.

Q: How much does an FOB cost?
A: Public conflict: $2,500 (allthings oil-rig) vs $7,500 (Season 1 changelog leads). Screenshot the vendor.

Q: What should I not buy first match?
A: Entire bankroll, solo Hot Zone vehicles, blind FOB drills, max armor before fundamentals, Artillery Tank — refine with verified prices.

Q: What is lost on death — kit, wallet, or both?
A: “Kit lost / bankroll kept” is a community clue, not a store sentence. We omit it as fact until reproduced. Store-explicit: cash persists match to match.
