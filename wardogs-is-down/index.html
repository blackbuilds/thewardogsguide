FAQ

Q: Is WARDOGS down right now?
A: We cannot stamp FIXED/UP forever from a static article. Run the 60-second table (Steam News / discussions / Discord / X / friends / your dialog). Deep context → Servers.

Q: Where do I check official status first?
A: Steam News for App 1867240, then https://x.com/WARDOGS and Discord invite https://discord.gg/TxKKdspkCp. Date-stamp what you see.

Q: I see JOIN TIMEDOUT / 1628505091 — is that “down”?
A: It’s a join-handshake timeout class — sometimes widespread, sometimes one server. Use 1628505091, not a reinstall.

Q: I see Failed to Authenticate / 1147405308 — is that a ban?
A: Public lead frames it as authenticate / online-services failure; launch-day reports were often service-wide. Not a ban by default. → 1147405308 and Servers.

Q: What about Patch 0.11 / Sep 14 maintenance?
A: Announced ~1 hour downtime for server-browser work — digest on Patch notes. Errors around that window are maintenance-adjacent until proven otherwise.

Q: WD-L014 on launch — are servers down?
A: Community treats WD-L014 / WD-LO14 as launcher/Elytra init — check status once, then WD-L014. Don’t only sit in queue.

Q: Should I reinstall if the game feels down?
A: Not as a first step during a global incident. Finish updates, wait, Steam Exit once, then branch by dialog.

Q: Is this the same as the Servers page?
A: No — this is the short status checklist. Servers remains the deep timeline hub.

Q: Does WARDOGS use Elytra or EAC?
A: Store feature tag as of 2026-09-13 editorial: Uses Kernel Level Anti-Cheat Elytra. Keep Elytra-first on launch failures.

Q: Can a VPN fix “servers down”?
A: Do not claim VPN as a studio fix for outages. Hotspot/VPN are diagnostic on local branches only (see timeout spoke).
