Equal-odds note (allthings): Default, Church, and River each have the same chance of being picked. Do not treat Default as “always” without a match screenshot.

Spacing lead (allthings): all four possible play-area positions sit close together — no more than ~693 m apart at the extremes — so every match is decided inside a small slice of the larger battlefield. Still against your HUD.

Default — busiest slice

Editorial paraphrase of allthings (Sep 13 2026): Default pulls residential streets, terraced hillside toward the church, pine stands, farm plots, shops, apartments/garages, church top, lumberyard, pool, and the football stadium complex (frontage / garages / parking). All four drill towers fall inside its radius — the code race often starts here. Town interior + stadium flank are the loudest fights.

Church — hold the climb

Church puts the objective on high ground under the named church. Elevation does the work: the team that holds the climb and the sightlines it opens tends to hold the zone. Open approaches below the terracing are exposed — budget cover routes, not ego YOLO. Dated lead: three towers in radius.

River — scarce towers

River is tighter and greener NW of centre. With only two towers in radius (dated), contesting both code sources matters more than in Default. Treelines and the river line give cover — and give recon somewhere to sit.

Farmland — transit only

Farmland is fully defined west of centre but never hosts the objective rotation (allthings). Cross it as a corridor. Knowing what it holds still helps: covered lines can shorten the trip to whichever live zone is active. Do not stage “Farmland hold” as the match plan.

Control Zone rules (what matters in an Ozeti fight)

The Control Zone is the match’s scoring theater, not merely “the middle of the map.”

Topic Guidance Notes
Store vs indie size Store ~2×2 km vs ~85 m scoring core inside ~550 m play area Resolve with screenshot scale
Three-team pressure Flank ≠ empty; third team may collapse your hold Store 3-team framing
Verticality Church climb + tower roofs dominate sightlines Live
Logistics No respawn countdown — die → HQ; travel time is the tax (allthings) Budget transport → Economy page

Control Zone presence checklist

  • [ ] Locate zone edge markers on HUD/map
  • [ ] Identify active play area name if shown (Default / Church / River)
  • [ ] Confirm you are not treating Farmland as the objective host
  • [ ] Identify nearest friendly FOB / spawn logic
  • [ ] Identify one tower, one settlement cluster, one industrial/stadium hardpoint
  • [ ] Agree with squad: hold vs roam vs Hot Zone contest
  • [ ] Watch score — leave ego fights that don’t buy ticks

First-match Ozeti orientation (10 minutes)

Use with How to Play:

  1. Read the map name on load — screenshot it (Ozeti?).
  2. Identify play area if listed (Default / Church / River — not Farmland as host).
  3. Open in-match map; find Control Zone outline.
  4. Ping one tower and one soft POI (settlement / stadium / shops).
  5. Buy ultra-budget kit — don’t empty bankroll (Economy).
  6. Agree a drop-off with driver/pilot before buying transport — die → HQ travel tax (allthings).
  7. Arrive with ammo; die once learning lanes is cheaper than a vehicle YOLO.
  8. Only contest Hot Zone if squad + reserve allow.
  9. After match, note which POI actually moved the score.

FAQ

Q: Is Ozeti playable in WARDOGS Early Access right now?
A: MetaForge hub capture 2026-09-13 lists Ozeti as Playable now (with Bakurani and Zestafona). Still confirm the load-screen UI name in your match before treating any third-party pin dump as live.

Q: How big is the Control Zone and how do you win on Ozeti?
A: Steam store About (2026-09-13): about 2×2 km, first to 100 points, ~100 players / 3 teams. Indie Ozeti guide: ~550 m play areas with ~85 m scoring cores — size conflict kept as public lead. Tick UI labels remain public lead until HUD screenshot.

Q: Which Ozeti play areas are in rotation?
A: Dated lead (allthings Sep 13 2026): Default, Church, River with equal odds. Farmland is defined but not selected as the objective host — transit only. MetaForge “four positions” wording soft-conflicts — note both.

Q: How many towers on Ozeti?
A: Depends on play area. Dated leads: Default 4, Church 3, River 2. Flat “Ozeti = 4” tables conflict — screenshot your match. Deep capture flow → Towers.

Q: Is this an interactive map / map tool?
A: No. This page is an editorial Ozeti names and Control Zone objectives guide only — not a clickable/filterable map tool. For interactive layers, use MetaForge / Ozeti map.

Q: How is Ozeti different from Bakurani?
A: Terrain and play-area set differ (Ozeti: Default/Church/River + Farmland transit; Bakurani: Default/Farmland/Lumberyard with different tower weights). Same win framing (store 100 points / Control Zone / Hot Zone cash). Sibling → Bakurani map.

Q: Does Hot Zone change the map?
A: It changes where the fight and cash multiplier concentrate. Pull/hold rules → Towers; cash → Economy page.

Q: Where should a new player go first on Ozeti?
A: Soft POI or team-tagged objective edge — not solo tower keypad. Learn Default town interior vs Church climb vs River scarce towers. See How to Play.

Q: Do maps affect starter loadout?
A: Indirectly (range vs CQB; transport budget on large travel — Ozeti punishes death with HQ travel tax per allthings). Keep first kits budget-safe until you know the lane.

Q: PC only?
A: This hub targets Steam PC EA per store. Console wishlist chatter is out of scope here.
