Editorial policy

How we source facts

  1. Steam store and official patch notes — first stop for mode, anti-cheat, economy framing, and patch timelines.
  2. Press — used when dated and attributed.
  3. Community guides — supporting context, not treated as confirmed until we can back them up.

What we leave out

If a price, keybind, menu label, or “fixed” claim is not confirmed, it does not appear on the page. We would rather publish less than guess.

Independence

Unofficial and independent. Not affiliated with BULKHEAD or Team17.
