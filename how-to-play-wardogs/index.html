TL;DR: WARDOGS (Steam EA since 2026-09-10) is a Windows PC milsim-scale FPS: ~100 players, 3 teams (Lonestar / Valkyra / Manticore in indie guides), fight inside a Control Zone until a team hits 100 points. Store economy: journey starts with $10,000; cash persists between matches; Hot Zone is marketed as double cash — full tower pull logic on Towers / Hot Zone. This page is a first-30-minutes checklist, not generic “100 beginner tips.” Crash → Crashing / Elytra. FPS → Best Settings. Bankroll → Economy.

Six progression tracks (after match 1)

games.gg beginner (Sep 10, 2026): no hard class lock — six tracks unlock gear via actions: Assault, Medic, Recon, Support, Driver, Pilot. Equipment you buy defines the role. New players: Assault (familiar FPS) or Medic (cash via revives/heals). Deep unlock prices stay on Economy.

FAQ

Q: How do I start my first WARDOGS match on Steam?
A: Launch → update client → queue or Server Browser in-region → spawn → buy cheap kit + ammo → move to Control Zone. Exact button labels.

Q: How do you win?
A: Be on the team that reaches 100 points in the Control Zone fight (store/press framing). Indie guides: most players in zone when the tick fills (~30s) — confirm.

Q: Does money carry over?
A: Steam store says cash persists match to match and the journey starts with $10,000. Confirm on your HUD across two matches before arguing reset myths.

Q: What is the Hot Zone for a brand-new player?
A: A double-cash (store) hotspot you should recognize early but not die for alone. Full capture/pull systems → Towers / Hot Zone.

Q: Should I wait for a revive or respawn?
A: Indie guides: revive keeps your kit and is usually cheaper. Confirm live.

Q: Is there a mandatory tutorial?
A: — many players learn by first match; Firing Range exists for vehicles/heli practice per public guides. Don’t skip Settings + ammo buy either way.

Q: Can I play solo?
A: Queuing alone into 100-player servers is expected; coordinated squads perform better. Exact party UI.

Q: PC only?
A: Windows PC Steam EA as of 2026-09-13 store check. Do not claim console release here.

Q: Reviews / price?
A: Snapshot: Very Positive ~85% / ~28,744 EN reviews (store check date). Game prices change — don’t hardcode a forever price in this guide.

Q: What anti-cheat does it use?
A: Store tag: Elytra (kernel level). Some third-party settings blogs mislabel AC — trust the store. Launch failures → Crash page.

Q: What should I learn after match 1?
A: games.gg progress checklist: find scoring area without double-checking map; notice Hot Zone moves; remember third faction; refill from cover; wait for revives; change one habit per death.
