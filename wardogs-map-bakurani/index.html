First 10 minutes on Bakurani

  1. Read load-screen name — confirm Bakurani (not Ozeti / Zestafona).
  2. Note play area if shown (UI).
  3. Open in-match map; find Control Zone outline.
  4. Ping one tower and one soft POI (settlement/industry).
  5. Decide: score hold vs Hot Zone cash plan.
  6. Budget a vehicle if HQ is far (Economy).
  7. Die once on purpose if learning travel tax — better now than mid-objective.
  8. Screenshot tower count before arguing Discord numbers.

FAQ

Q: Is Bakurani playable in WARDOGS Early Access?
A: MetaForge hub capture (2026-09-13) lists Bakurani Playable now. Confirm on your load screen.

Q: How big is the Control Zone and how do you win?
A: Steam store About (2026-09-13): Control Zone ~2×2 km, first to ~100 points, ~100 players / 3 teams. Indie guides cite a smaller scoring core — keep the size conflict visible until HUD screenshots resolve it.

Q: How many towers does Bakurani have?
A: Conflict. Source A (allthings towers Sep 12): 7 (+4 abandoned). Source B (allthings Bakurani Sep 13): 5 / 5 / 3 by play area. Prefer dated play-area counts + your screenshot. Abandoned do not count as code sources per overall table.

Q: Is this an interactive Bakurani map?
A: No. This is editorial. Interactive pins → MetaForge.

Q: What’s the difference between Control Zone and Hot Zone?
A: Control Zone = scoring theater toward 100 points. Hot Zone = store double-cash (and tool leads for presence) usually tied to tower drill flow — see Towers.

Q: Bakurani vs Ozeti — which should I learn first?
A: Whichever your server loads. Ozeti has its own play-area table (4/3/2 leads) on Ozeti map and the Maps hub.

Q: Do abandoned towers still give digits?
A: allthings overall table: abandoned do not count. on your build before wasting a push.

Q: Where do I learn the drill / keypad / Hot Zone move?
A: WARDOGS Towers and Hot Zone.

Q: First match tips beyond the map?
A: How to Play + Economy starter loadout.

Q: Why does MetaForge sometimes say Kavkazi?
A: Tool naming can lag or use internal/legacy labels. Trust your in-match load screen for the live map name; keep MetaForge for pins, not as the sole naming authority.

Q: Does Bakurani use the same Hot Zone rules as Ozeti?
A: Store Hot Zone cash framing is game-wide. Play-area tower density differs — learn digits on Towers and map-specific counts here vs Ozeti.
