Play areas (allthings Sep 13, 2026)

Four play areas are defined; only three host the objective. Each is a ~500 m circle with an ~85 m Control Zone inside (allthings). Furthest pair cited ~531 m apart — rotations can drag the fight across the built-up core.

Play area Position lead In rotation? Character (editorial paraphrase) Drill towers (dated)
Water Treatment North of the middle Yes Most open of the three; warehouses + woodland, factories, offices/residential, towers, train sheds/tracks, water towers, bridge, scrap + large/long shipping yards Unknown / unverified
Houses South-west of the middle Yes Heaviest cover — warehousing + thickest woodland, towers, brick warehouses, factories, residential/apartments, shops, train sheds, bridge, bus depot, scrap, long shipping yard Unknown / unverified
Small Factory South-east of the middle No — scenery / transit only Defined circle but objective never lands here (allthings) Do not stage as objective host

Equal-frequency note (allthings): Default, Water Treatment, and Houses share rotation odds. Do not treat Default as “always” without a match screenshot.

Spacing lead (allthings): Two furthest play areas ~531 m apart — budget travel tax (die → HQ, no countdown).

Default — densest industrial slice

Editorial paraphrase of allthings (Sep 13, 2026): Default east of centre packs the most named ground — warehouse rows vs open yard lanes, with towers giving recon lines over the circle. Expect CQB inside warehouses and longer sightlines across yards / farmland strip.

Water Treatment — open lanes + tracks

North of centre; fewest packed named pieces. Train tracks and water towers create marksman lines; Hot Zone can pull knots toward whichever corner has cover (allthings paraphrase).

Houses — CQB / woodland hold

South-west; thickest woodland + residential streets. Head-on pushes into defended blocks get punished — prefer covered approaches (allthings paraphrase).

Small Factory — out of rotation

South-east; treat as scenery / corridor knowledge, not the match plan. Knowing its landmarks still helps rotations when the live zone is elsewhere.

First 10 minutes on Zestafona

  1. Read load-screen name — confirm Zestafona (not Bakurani / Ozeti).
  2. Note play area if shown (Default / Water Treatment / Houses — not Small Factory as host).
  3. Open in-match map; find Control Zone outline.
  4. Ping one tower and one soft POI (warehouse cluster / apartments / rail shed).
  5. Decide: score hold vs Hot Zone cash plan.
  6. Budget a vehicle if HQ is far — die → HQ travel tax (allthings).
  7. Prefer woodland / warehouse interiors over open yard YOLO into recon (allthings approach tip).
  8. Screenshot tower count before arguing Discord numbers.

FAQ

Q: Is Zestafona playable in WARDOGS Early Access?
A: MetaForge hub capture (2026-09-13) lists Zestafona Playable now (supersedes earlier “not yet in rotation” copy). Public hub still frames Bakurani / Ozeti / Zestafona as playable (editorial 2026-09-21). Confirm on your load screen.

Q: How big is the Control Zone and how do you win?
A: Steam store About (2026-09-13): Control Zone ~2×2 km, first to ~100 points, ~100 players / 3 teams. allthings Zestafona cites ~500 m play-area circles with ~85 m scoring cores — keep the size conflict visible until HUD screenshots resolve it.

Q: What are the Zestafona play areas?
A: allthings (Sep 13, 2026): Default (east), Water Treatment (north), Houses (south-west) rotate at equal frequency; Small Factory (south-east) is defined but out of objective rotation.

Q: How many towers does Zestafona have?
A: Unverified as a fixed number. allthings discusses towers without a Bakurani/Ozeti-style per-area count table. MetaForge’s Zestafona blurb mentions three live tower objectives as a tool lead — screenshot your match. Prefer Towers for drill flow.

Q: Is this an interactive Zestafona map?
A: No. This is editorial — not a map tool. Interactive pins → MetaForge hub · Zestafona interactive.

Q: What’s the difference between Control Zone and Hot Zone?
A: Control Zone = scoring theater toward 100 points. Hot Zone = store double-cash (and tool leads for double presence) usually tied to tower drill flow — see Towers.

Q: Zestafona vs Bakurani vs Ozeti — which should I learn first?
A: Whichever your server loads. Bakurani is the compact valley sibling; Ozeti the Western Europe river basin; Zestafona the North America industrial river-bend. See Maps hub, Bakurani, Ozeti.

Q: Where do I learn the drill / keypad / Hot Zone move?
A: WARDOGS Towers and Hot Zone.
